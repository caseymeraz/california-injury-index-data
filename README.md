## What is included

`firms.csv` records the published rank, firm name, California market, editorial best-for category, recognition signal, California Injury Index profile URL, recognition source, firm-reported results source, and official website for each of the ten firms in the September 30, 2026 edition.

The dataset distinguishes third-party recognition from firm-reported results. A large recovery figure is not treated as independently audited unless the cited source provides that verification.

## Methodology and limitations

The ranking is an editorial research starting point, not legal advice, a referral, or a guarantee that a firm is appropriate for a particular case. Rankings consider the quality and inspectability of public evidence, relevant professional recognition, published results, California presence, team depth, client evidence, professional standing, and transparency.

See the complete, current methodology at [bestcaliforniainjurylawyers.com/methodology](https://bestcaliforniainjurylawyers.com/methodology).
